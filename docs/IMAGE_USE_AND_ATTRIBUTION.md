# Product Image Use and Attribution

This document applies to the Starlite Tools digital-showroom pitch and is kept
inside the project repository. It is not website copy and must not be rendered
in the public interface.

## Pitch-stage image disclaimer

Product photographs used or referenced by this demo may be sourced from INGCO product
pages, Pinterest pins, authorised or independent tool retailers, and other
publicly accessible catalogue listings. Those images remain the property of
their respective owners. Starlite Tools and the demo authors do not claim
ownership of third-party product photography, brand marks, packaging, or trade
dress.

The images are included temporarily to demonstrate catalogue structure, product
discovery, quoting, and sales workflows. Their inclusion does not imply
endorsement, sponsorship, authorisation, or an official relationship with the
image owner or source platform.

If a rights holder, supplier, retailer, platform, or the client requests removal
or replacement of an image, the project team will remove or replace it promptly.
Before a production launch, Starlite Tools should obtain an approved supplier
media pack or other appropriate permission and replace temporary external image
links with approved, locally managed assets.

## Source tracking

The exact source page, direct image URL, and locally staged pitch asset used for
each mapped SKU are recorded in `lib/product-image-links.generated.json`.
Generated product cutouts in
`public/images/tools/` are retained only as resilient fallbacks when an external
image is unavailable.

## Publication checklist

- Confirm written permission or an approved manufacturer/dealer media pack.
- Replace temporary hotlinks with approved, locally managed files.
- Verify that each image matches the exact SKU, model, and included accessories.
- Remove retailer pricing, promotions, watermarks, and marketplace-specific copy.
- Keep source and approval records with the catalogue data.
